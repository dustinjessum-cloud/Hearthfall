## What this is

A self-contained Phaser 3 RTS/town-builder that runs from `file://` — no build
step, no server. Open `index.html` and it plays.

~9,500 lines across `js/*.js`. Art is generated by `gen_sprites.py` (Python +
PIL) into a spritesheet that is base64-embedded into `js/content.js`.

**Four factions**, all playable from the start screen:

| Faction | Identity | Economy |
|---|---|---|
| **Human** | The baseline town-builder | 7 resources, wheat→flour→bread chain, happiness, gold, trade |
| **Undead** | Blight, corpses, bone | Carrion + bone; blight gates all building placement |
| **Tribe** | Ogres and hobgoblins | Hunts near forest, no cooking chain, plants trees |
| **Grove** | A living forest network | Structures yield by being *connected*; Ents tend, and can gather badly |

**The arc:** survive 5 raids → mountain passes open → race a real AI economy
for a rich neutral zone → raze their Town Hall to win.

**The enemy town plays one of the four factions too**, drawn at world creation
and never your own — and an undead or Grove enemy plays by that faction's own
rules, not a reskinned human one. See below. None of it has been seen in a
real playthrough yet.

## Non-obvious things that will bite you

These are all hard-won. Read before changing anything nearby.

- **`FRAME` in `content.js` is GENERATED** from `gen_sprites.py`'s `DRAWERS`
  order. Never hand-edit it, and never insert a sprite mid-list — that shifts
  every index after it, and a stale entry renders the *wrong sprite* rather
  than failing. Append to the END of `DRAWERS` and regenerate.
- **Sprite budget is at 100 of 102.** The next sprite means bumping `ROWS` in
  `gen_sprites.py` **and** `rows` in `main.js` `setupFrames()` **together**.
  They must always move as a pair.
- **After ANY art change**: run `python gen_sprites.py`, re-embed the base64
  into `content.js`, rebuild `FRAME` from `DRAWERS`, then run
  `python stamp_assets.py` (cache-busts the script tags). Skipping the stamp
  means the browser serves stale JS.
- **`applyFaction()` mutates shared globals and never resets**, so a page load
  hosts exactly one faction. Switching requires a reload. This is why testing
  two factions in one session gives nonsense.
- **`FACTION_DEFS` validates itself at load** against the human entry's keys.
  A new faction missing a field logs a named error instead of throwing deep
  inside an unrelated helper.
- **`state.buildings` holds BOTH sides.** Use `myBuildings()` / `aiBuildings()`.
  Touching the raw array is almost always the bug. Anything that re-skins a
  building must also check `isMine(b)` — `evolutionFrameFor()` did not, and
  restamped your core's sprite onto the enemy's on every reload.
- **AI faction vs AI race are DIFFERENT vocabularies.** `aiTownFaction()`
  returns `human|swarm|tribe|grove` and drives art, names and roster.
  `aiTownRace()` returns `human|undead|troll` and is what `ENEMY_RACES` is
  indexed by and what the undead-only blight checks test. Collapsing them
  gives the tribe undead stat multipliers, or starts the tribe blighting.
- **Unit behaviour comes from `UNIT_DEFS`**, not string comparisons. Add a row
  there rather than another `u.type==='archer' || ...` chain.
- **The in-app browser preview refuses to reload `file://`.** Use the local
  server (below). If test results look impossible, check the `?v=` stamps on
  the script tags — a cached `index.html` serves stale JS.

## Running and testing it

- `.claude/launch.json` starts `python -m http.server 8123`. Serving over
  http is the only reliable way to get a real reload. **`.claude/` is
  untracked** — it also holds your local settings, so it was deliberately
  never committed.
- Navigating with a changing query string (`index.html?cb=7`) forces a fresh
  document when the pane caches one.
- **Browser downloads do reach `~/Downloads`.** That is the reliable way to
  get a rendered PNG out of the running game — far cheaper than passing image
  data back through the tool layer.
- **Read exported logs with a script, not by eye.** A 46-minute run is ~2,800
  lines of JSON; a five-line Python script that tabulates the snapshots tells
  you far more, far cheaper.
- **Two testing buttons** (purple, top bar): **Sandbox** keeps resources at
  cap, **Skip to Corridor** jumps straight to the endgame.
- **Session recorder**: red `● REC ON · n` in the top bar; **Export Log**
  downloads the run as JSON. It has caught starvation, frozen production, an
  unbuildable food building, and a 32-minute AI stall.

## Where each faction stands

**Human** — complete and the most mature. Two known traps: training a soldier
consumes the villager *instantly* but the soldier takes 60s to appear; and the
opening can stall outright (see issue 1).

**Undead** — blight spreads manually (select a blight structure → "Spread
Blight"). Necropolis gets 1 seeding, Grave Mounds 2, capped at 2 generations.
Ritual Pit banks 20 corpses → Flesh Golem. **Now has walls**: Bone Fence
(8 carrion, 70hp) reinforced per segment to a Crypt Wall (12 bone, 180hp).

**Tribe** — hunting economy, Seed Grove (Forester plants 10 saplings on a
5-minute cooldown), stake-wall art. Signature mechanic still **open**.

**Grove** — structures are placed anywhere, lie dormant as Seeds, and a root
creeps to them from the nearest point on the network. Severed = no production
*and* no growth, and severing cascades. **Ents can now gather** — Heartroot and
Stonebark are both a passive yield and a workplace — but badly (1.8× the time,
0.6× the load, 0.75× speed), and only when you send them by hand. A gathering
Ent is outside `tendRadius`, so it stops adding its +25%. That trade is the point.

## The enemy town

Plays one of the four factions, drawn per run, never your own. It builds,
trains and looks like that faction. Tribe and Grove reuse exactly the frames
those factions use for themselves, placeholders included.

**Undead and Grove enemies play by their own rules.** Per-faction behaviour
lives in `AI_FACTION_RULES` in `ai.js` rather than in scattered
`aiTownFaction()==='swarm'` branches:

- **Undead** — carrion is its only resource (a `costMap` collapses wood and
  stone into food, and its workers render forest rather than cutting timber);
  every structure consumes a drone, never below a floor of 3; it can only
  build on blight, prefers the frontier tile nearest the middle so the stain
  grows toward you, and may build in the OPEN pass because its territory has
  to be physically continuous.
- **Grove** — structures are inert until a root reaches them and then pay out
  for being connected, not staffed. Placement respects `rootMaxLen`;
  structures that fail to root at birth retry as the network grows toward
  them, while ones whose root was CUT stay cut. Cutting its trunk cascades
  against it exactly as it does against you.
- **Tribe** — still on the human profile. Hunting is the last piece (step 4).

`state.creep` and `state.groveRoots` are each shared by whichever side is
using them, which is only safe because **a mirror match cannot happen** — the
enemy's faction is drawn from the three you are not playing, so at most one
side is ever undead and at most one is ever Grove. Add a mirror mode and both
become two networks in one container.

Raid waves are a **separate system** and still use the old `oppositeRace()`
troll/undead logic rather than following the AI's drawn faction. Left alone
deliberately so tuned raid balance was not disturbed.

## Known open issues (priority order)

1. **The human opening can stall completely.** `AUTO_ASSIGN_RADIUS` is 4, and
   `autoAssignIdleVillagers()` is event-driven with no periodic retry — so a
   foundation 5.8 tiles from an idle villager is never built, and it never
   retries. Measured in a normal opening: wood income zero for a whole run
   while upkeep drained the pile. Two small changes; deliberately deferred.
2. **Nothing built since the AI rework has been seen in a real game.** Every
   number below comes from simulated runs, not play. Enemy building counts by
   minute 24: human ~59, Grove ~77, undead ~110. A Grove enemy banks huge
   inert piles (food only buys units and its army caps at 22 by minute eight;
   stone it cannot spend at all) and is structurally immune to worker
   harassment — correct for the faction, but it means cutting roots is the
   only counterplay. An undead enemy's single-resource economy never hits the
   have-wood-need-stone stalls that gate the others. **Play a run and export
   the log before tuning any of this.**
3. **Tribe AI mechanics** (step 4) — hunting camps and Foresters. The last of
   the four; the scaffolding and rules table are already in place for it.
4. **The Heartwood cannot grow past Ancient.**
   `groveMaxStage()` computes `min(heartwoodMaxStage: 5, stages.length-1: 3)`
   = 3, identical to the ordinary cap. It maxes out 45s in, so "the oldest
   tree is always the heart" is unimplemented. Needs two more stage entries.
5. **The Grove's second Ent is unaffordable when it matters.** 60 food + 40
   wood; in a recorded run both were first affordable at t=210 — the exact
   moment wave 1 landed. The faction played the whole run on one unit and lost
   it to the first raid.
6. **The Grove's stone is unspendable.** Stonebark produces it; every Grove
   building costs wood only.
7. **Undead wood and stone are dead resources** — should be hidden from their HUD.
8. **Every undead building consumes a drone**, so population oscillates while
   the cap climbs. The faction can't grow.
9. **`updateUnits()` is still ~300 lines** of interleaved movement/gather/
   build/combat. The remaining big refactor. Use brace-MATCHED extraction,
   never hardcoded line numbers.
10. **No sound at all.** Biggest perceived-quality gap.
11. Grove's Heartroot, Stonebark and Thornhall still wear tribe art (the
    player's versions do too, so at least they match).

## How we've been working

- **Art is always shown before committing.** Render a comparison PNG and send
  it — tile-sprites must be shown *tiled*, and everything must also be checked
  at true 32px. Several passes were rejected for looking fine at 8× and wrong
  at true size; one was rejected in review for reading as a heap of eyeballs.
- **Balance is argued from logs, not vibes.** There is far more built than
  validated — that is the standing tension in this project.
- **Verify in a running game, not by reading.** Most of the real bugs this
  project has hit were invisible in the source and obvious the moment
  something was actually driven: the AI's 32-minute stall, the Grove's roots
  dying on reload, `Continue` throwing on any save with a moving enemy worker.
