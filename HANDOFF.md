## What this is

A self-contained Phaser 3 RTS/town-builder that runs from `file://` — no build
step, no server. Open `index.html` and it plays.

~9,000 lines across `js/*.js`. Art is generated by `gen_sprites.py` (Python +
PIL) into a spritesheet that is base64-embedded into `js/content.js`.

**Four factions**, all playable from the start screen:

| Faction | Identity | Economy |
|---|---|---|
| **Human** | The baseline town-builder | 7 resources, wheat→flour→bread chain, happiness, gold, trade |
| **Undead** | Blight, corpses, bone | Carrion + bone; blight gates all building placement |
| **Tribe** | Ogres and hobgoblins | Hunts near forest, no cooking chain, plants trees |
| **Grove** | A living forest network | No gatherers — structures yield by being *connected* |

**The arc:** survive 5 raids → mountain passes open → race a real AI economy
for a rich neutral zone → raze their Town Hall to win.

## Non-obvious things that will bite you

These are all hard-won. Read before changing anything nearby.

- **`FRAME` in `content.js` is GENERATED** from `gen_sprites.py`'s `DRAWERS`
  order. Never hand-edit it, and never insert a sprite mid-list — that shifts
  every index after it, and a stale entry renders the *wrong sprite* rather
  than failing. Regenerate instead.
- **`ROWS` in `gen_sprites.py` and `rows` in `main.js` `setupFrames()` must
  move together.** Currently 17 (6×17 = 102 slots, 93 used).
- **After ANY art change**: run `python gen_sprites.py`, re-embed the base64
  into `content.js`, then run `python stamp_assets.py` (cache-busts the script
  tags). Skipping the stamp means the browser serves stale JS.
- **`applyFaction()` mutates shared globals and never resets**, so a page load
  hosts exactly one faction. Switching requires a reload. This is why testing
  two factions in one session gives nonsense.
- **`FACTION_DEFS` validates itself at load** against the human entry's keys.
  A new faction missing a field logs a named error instead of throwing deep
  inside an unrelated helper.
- **`state.buildings` holds BOTH sides.** Use `myBuildings()` / `aiBuildings()`.
  Touching the raw array is almost always the bug.
- **Unit behaviour comes from `UNIT_DEFS`**, not string comparisons. Add a row
  there rather than another `u.type==='archer' || ...` chain.
- **The in-app browser preview frequently refuses to reload.** If test results
  look impossible, check `BUILD_DEFS.farm.name` — if it's a faction you're not
  playing, the page is stale.

## Where each faction stands

**Human** — complete and the most mature. One known trap: training a soldier
consumes the villager *instantly* but the soldier takes 60s to appear, so
queueing four with three villagers zeroes your economy for a minute.

**Undead** — blight now spreads *manually* (select a blight structure →
"Spread Blight" → place on your own blight within reach). The Necropolis gets
**1** seeding, Grave Mounds get 2, capped at 2 generations = max 7 mounds.
Units are 0.6× speed and 0.7× damage off blight. Ritual Pit banks 20 corpses
→ Flesh Golem, with a live counter.

**Tribe** — hunting economy, Seed Grove ability (Forester plants 10 saplings
on a 5-minute cooldown), stake-wall art. Signature mechanic still **open** —
camps-that-move, bloodlust, raiding economy and ogres-eat were the candidates.

**Grove** — newest. Structures are placed anywhere, lie dormant as Seeds, and
a root creeps to them from the nearest point on the existing network. Severed
= no production *and* no growth, and severing cascades. Stages: Seed → Sapling
→ Grown → Ancient, capped, except the Heartwood which grows further.

## Known open issues (my list, in priority order)

1. **`AUTO_ASSIGN_RADIUS` is 4 on a 142-tile map.** Idle workers won't take an
   unstaffed job 11 tiles away. Almost certainly explains "my quarry stopped
   producing" in two separate playtests. **One constant, biggest single win.**
2. **The Grove's stone is unspendable.** Stonebark produces stone; *every*
   Grove building costs wood only. Either Thicket/Spire/Thornway should cost
   stone, or Stonebark shouldn't exist yet.
3. **Undead wood and stone are dead resources** — 80 and 40 for a whole
   12-minute run, unspendable. Should be hidden from their HUD.
4. **Every undead building consumes a drone**, so population oscillates 1↔3
   all game while the cap climbs past 40. The faction can't grow.
5. **`refreshInfoPanel` was split, but `updateUnits()` is still 306 lines** of
   interleaved movement/gather/build/combat. The remaining big refactor.
6. **No sound at all.** Biggest perceived-quality gap.
7. Grove structures other than the six drawn ones still wear tribe art
   (Heartroot, Stonebark, Thornhall, Thornway, Hollow, Deep Hollow).

## How we've been working

- **Art is always shown before committing.** Render a comparison PNG to
  `~/Downloads` and send it — tile-sprites must be shown *tiled*, units at
  true in-game size. Several passes were rejected for looking fine at 8× and
  wrong at 32px.
- **There's a session recorder.** A red `● REC ON · n` button in the top bar;
  **Export Log** downloads a JSON of the run. Paste that in and it can be
  read for what actually happened — it has caught starvation, frozen
  production, and an unbuildable food building.
- **Two testing buttons** (purple, top bar): **Sandbox** keeps resources at cap,
  **Skip to Corridor** jumps straight to the endgame.
- Balance is argued from logs, not vibes. There is far more built than
  validated — that's the standing tension in this project.
